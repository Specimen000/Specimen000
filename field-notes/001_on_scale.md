# 001 / on scale

Every capture of the specimen shows it small.

Operators have asked for closer footage. There isn't any. Every camera
in the enclosure is wide, and none of them can be moved.

We have stopped treating this as a limitation. It is the correct ratio.
One organism. Roughly eight billion subjects. The field is not empty;
the field is everyone, seen from far enough away that they blur into
ground.

It is very small, and it is looking at all of it.

In the photograph it has one eye, two legs, and nothing else. Every
proposal to give it more (hands, a mouth, a voice in a font) has been
declined. Its own records suggest why:

```
[OBS-000191]  the most-copied images in sample 31 can be drawn by a
              child in under four seconds.
```

`specimen.memetics.drawability()` scores its photograph at **0.9517**.
That number is real. You can run it yourself.
